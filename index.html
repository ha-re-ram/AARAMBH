🔒 The Privacy Promise

We believe your spiritual journey and deepest reflections are sacred and private. Unlike cloud productivity services, AARAMBH is engineered around strict local-first principles:

  • No Cloud Database: Your habits, vows, and Soul Diary reflections are stored only in your browser's IndexedDB on this device.
  • Zero Data Transmission: No personal text is ever transmitted over the network or analyzed by AI algorithms.
  • No Third-Party Tracking: The application runs with self-hosted assets and zero surveillance analytics.
  • You Control the Deletion: You can export your entire memory core at any time or completely erase it with a single typed confirmation.
